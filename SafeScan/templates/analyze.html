Risk Scale Policy
0 – 30 SAFE: Legitimate banking PSP handles, standard formatting, and absence of phishing indicators.
31 – 70 SUSPICIOUS: Masked links, unverified PSP handles, or promotional cashback wording detected.
71 – 100 HIGH RISK: Hostile IP hosts, malicious redirects, unencrypted HTTP, or deceptive extortion/KYC threats.
Security Best Practice

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. UPI PIN is exclusively entered when debiting your account. Any QR claiming to "credit cashback" that asks for your PIN is a guaranteed scam.
